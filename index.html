HOW TO PLAY

Space: sword dash in your facing direction. Shift is disabled. A kill reloads instantly; a missed strike costs 1 second. Chain kills within 2.5 seconds for a combo. F: shoot; shooting kills also reload immediately. P / Escape: pause. R: restart. Shield coins block a hit, magnets pull gold, and speed coins give a 5-second boost.

2P: P1 uses WASD, Space and F. P2 uses arrows, Enter and L. Separate lives and scores; shared timer. M switches mode and restarts.
